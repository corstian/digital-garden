Let me start this writing with a critique about the current state of the IT industry. It had been that with the introduction of large language models a large portion of the industry collectively decided to throw the concept of quality overboard in favour of a perceived improvement in velocity. It may be obvious in hindsight these people merely played lip service to the concept of quality without any understanding about underlying principles whatsoever. That is understandable in an industry where a perceived understanding of a thing results both in a lot of shiny coin as well as respect and recognition of peers. Such dynamics can be more breadly observed in society as well. This is a systematic issue we cannot merely attribute to individuals. At a systemic level there is a complex dynamic involving at least psychological, cultural, social and political factors at play.

The critique is not so much about the systemic influence on the IT sector, although I have my opinions about that. Neither is this critique about the individuals getting swept along into each days madness. Instead I would want to focus on the concepts of quality, abstractions, reasoning and people.
