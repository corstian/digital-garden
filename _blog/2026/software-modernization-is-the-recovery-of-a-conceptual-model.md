title: "Software modernization is the recovery of a conceptual model"
date: 2025-05-06
toc: false
---

Software can both be unmaintainably complex as well as reasonably simple depending on the way it had been expressed. As part of my work modernizing legacy applications it is possible to experience these seemingly opposing impressions at the same time. Oftentimes these stare you in the face around the transition between two expressions of the same underlying models. In extreme cases even this can be the difference between "this is the culmination of ten years of work" and "this could be built in a day". This ofcourse should not be taken literally. The technical expression of a conceptual model is the easy part. The hard work is with uncovering what is worth building in the first place. After that comes communication about what people - and consequently software as well - are all worked up about. It is the gradual development of this conceptual model where the real work was at all this time.

My work as a modernization worker is in uncovering this conceptual model and bringing forth clarity in the expression of this model. A cleaner and leaner implementation of the software is merely part of achieving such thing.
